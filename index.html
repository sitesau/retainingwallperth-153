Retaining Wall Builders Perth Pros and Cons Explained

When engaging retaining wall builders in Perth, the focus is on the ground being retained and the approval complexities arising from height, boundaries, and adjacent loads. Before delving into aesthetic finishes, it's crucial to document the retained height, whether the wall is situated on or near a boundary, if it supports a driveway or structure, and if neighbouring properties rely on it. While an overview of supporting Perth retaining walls can provide initial orientation, a builder will require specific site details. Photographs, when accompanied by measurements, drainage indicators, and access notes, are invaluable.

It's important to measure the approximate retained height, not just the visible face of the wall. Note any fences, the ground levels of neighbouring properties, and whether the wall is tiered. Look for signs of distress such as bulging, cracking, soil erosion, or water pooling behind the wall. Clearly communicate information about machine access, driveway width, and potential material placement areas.

Matching Wall Systems to Your Site

The primary building systems commonly used in Perth are limestone block and post-and-panel concrete. Limestone blocks are often favoured for sandy, limestone-rich Swan Coastal Plain sites and blend well with local streetscapes. Post-and-panel systems, which use precast concrete panels slotted between posts, offer a uniform finish and are frequently employed for boundary and driveway retaining walls.

Other systems serve more specialised purposes. Timber sleeper walls are suitable for garden terracing and low boundary retaining. Rock and boulder walls are ideal for hilly terrain and sites with granite outcrops, providing a rugged, natural aesthetic. Besser block walls are positioned for taller, engineered structural applications and can be rendered for a refined finish. The key question when selecting a material is its suitability for the required height, load-bearing capacity, drainage strategy, and desired aesthetic.

Navigating Approvals and Engineering

Generally, a local government building permit is required in Perth once a retaining wall holds back more than 0.5 metres of ground. However, a permit may be necessary for walls of any height if they are connected to other building works, impact or protect adjoining land, or affect a boundary wall or dividing fence.

For engineered walls, reference is made to AS 4678 and the requirement for a WA-registered building engineering practitioner. In such cases, a simple area-based rate is insufficient. Enquire about who is responsible for the drawings, permit information, engineering inputs, and the lodgement process with local government. Clarify how the retained height will be measured for the specific site, whether tiered walls will be assessed collectively, and if the wall will affect a boundary fence or adjacent land. Ensure you understand what engineering and permit documentation will be provided when required.

Integrating Drainage into Your Quote

Drainage is considered an integral part of the retaining wall system. This typically involves ag-drainage pipes, gravel backfill, and geofabric placed behind the wall. The importance of detailed drainage is amplified in Perth's climate, particularly during winter when sandy ground can become saturated.

Therefore, a comprehensive quote should clearly outline what measures will be taken behind the visible wall face. Ask about the discharge point for collected water, the specifications for the backfill material, and whether existing drainage systems will be reused or replaced. For repairs, the initial step involves accurate diagnosis. The guide highlights that drainage issues, footing condition, and the material's state require assessment, as a leaning timber section presents different challenges compared to a bulging block wall that might necessitate partial demolition and reconstruction.

  3. Request Detailed Quotes. Ensure each quote specifies the material, drainage system, excavation, backfill, engineering responsibilities, and permit handling.
  4. Review Drainage Plans. Confirm details of the ag-drainage, gravel, geofabric, and discharge points before the visible wall face is constructed.

Common questions

What is the permit trigger height for retaining walls in Perth? In Perth, a local government building permit is generally required for retaining walls that retain more than 0.5 metres of ground. However, walls of any height may require a permit if they affect adjoining land, boundaries, or are part of other building works.

What are the most common retaining wall systems in Perth? The two most common systems are limestone block walls, often seen on the Swan Coastal Plain, and post-and-panel concrete walls, frequently used for boundary and driveway applications.

Why is drainage so important for retaining walls? Proper drainage behind a retaining wall is crucial to prevent the build-up of hydrostatic pressure from saturated soil, which can lead to wall failure. This is particularly important in Perth's climate with its sandy soils.
